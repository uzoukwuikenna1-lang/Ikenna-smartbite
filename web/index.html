Everything a campus needs to eat

Built around four roles that work together in real time — so an order placed in a hostel reaches the kitchen, the rider, and back to you, live.

Order in a few taps

Browse campus vendors, build your cart, add a note for the kitchen (allergies, less pepper) and check out.

Live order tracking

Watch your order move from placed → accepted → preparing → on the way, updated the moment the vendor or rider acts.

Delivery PIN

Each order gets a secret 4-digit PIN. The rider can only mark it delivered once you read out the code — proof the food reached you.

Safety first

Verified riders (KYC)

Riders complete an identity check — ID plus a face scan — before they can deliver, so you always know who’s at your door.

Safety first

Simple payments

Pay by card, bank transfer or cash on delivery through a familiar checkout flow (demo mode for this build).

Dashboards for everyone

Vendors manage menus & orders, riders track earnings and deliveries, and admins oversee the whole platform.

From hostel to hand-off

Four steps, four people, one smooth delivery.

You order

Pick a vendor, add items and a note, choose where to deliver, and pay.

Vendor prepares

The kitchen accepts, cooks your order and marks it ready for pickup.

Rider delivers

A verified rider claims the order, picks it up and heads to your location.

PIN confirms it

You read out your delivery PIN — only then is the order marked complete.
